How often should you check a Czech supplier's VAT status?

Updated 22 October 2026 · about 3 minutes

The dates that matter

Under §109(1) of the VAT Act, the question is what you knew or should have known when the obligation to declare the VAT arose, and, where the VAT became due on supply, also when you paid. The presumptions in §109(2), such as an unreliable payer or an unpublished account, are tested at those moments.

What that means in practice

  • A check when you sign the contract is not enough.
  • Check on the day of each payment, ideally right before the payment run.
  • Keep a dated record (printout, screenshot or report) of each check.

Doing it without effort

The VAT register is updated daily. For a handful of suppliers a manual check before each payment works. For more, an automatic daily check with alerts saves time and gives you a dated record.
