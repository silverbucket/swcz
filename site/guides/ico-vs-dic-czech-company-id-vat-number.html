IČO vs DIČ: the Czech company ID and VAT number

Updated 22 October 2026 · about 3 minutes

IČODIČ
What it isIdentification number of a person or businessTax identification number; used as the VAT number
Format8 digits, the last one a check digit"CZ" followed by 8 to 10 digits
Used forBusiness registers (ARES, public register, ISIR)VAT register, invoices, VIES

How they relate

For a legal entity the digits after "CZ" are normally the same as its IČO, unless the tax office assigned a special number. For an individual (sole trader) the DIČ is usually based on the birth number, not the IČO. This is how the VAT register help in English describes it.

Finding one from the other

  • Have the IČO? Look it up in ARES, Ministry of Finance; the record shows the DIČ if one exists.
  • Have the DIČ? Search it in the VAT register (Registr plátců DPH), Financial Administration, or check it across the EU in VIES.

A company with an IČO but no DIČ in ARES is probably not a VAT payer. Then the §109 rules on unreliable payers do not apply to purchases from it.
