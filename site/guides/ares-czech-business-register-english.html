ARES, the Czech business register, explained in English

Updated 22 October 2026 · about 4 minutes

ARES (Administrativní registr ekonomických subjektů) is run by the Ministry of Finance. It brings together data about businesses from several public registers, including the public (commercial) register, the trade licence register and the VAT register. It is free and needs no account.

How to search

  1. Open ARES, Ministry of Finance.
  2. Type the IČO (8 digits) or the company name.
  3. Open the result to see the record and links to the source registers.

What ARES does not tell you

ARES shows who the company is. For VAT reliability and bank accounts use the VAT register (Registr plátců DPH), Financial Administration; for insolvency use ISIR. Address, director or name changes can be a sign of trouble, see liquidation.
