The Czech insolvency register (ISIR) in English

Updated 22 October 2026 · about 5 minutes

ISIR (insolvenční rejstřík) is the public register of insolvency proceedings, run by the Ministry of Justice (§419 of the Insolvency Act). It holds a list of debtors and the insolvency file for each one, and it is free to search.

How to search

  1. Open Insolvency register ISIR, Ministry of Justice.
  2. Enter the supplier's IČO (or name) and search.
  3. If a proceeding exists, open it to see the file: the petition, court decisions and their dates.

Terms you will see

CzechEnglish
Insolvenční návrhInsolvency petition. Proceedings start on the day it reaches the court (§97(1)).
ÚpadekInsolvency (the debtor is found insolvent)

KonkursBankruptcy (liquidation of assets)

What it means for you

A petition is not proof that the supplier is insolvent; some are rejected. But it is an early warning: think twice about advance payments, long warranties or big orders, and if the supplier owes you money, deadlines for filing claims apply. Ask a lawyer about those.
