Czech supplier due diligence checklist

Updated 22 October 2026 · about 4 minutes

Every check below uses a free official register and takes a few minutes. The point of the checklist is timing: some checks matter once, before you sign, and others matter before every payment, because that is when §109 liability is tested.

Before the first order: identity, signing authority, VAT and insolvency

CheckWhere
The company exists, the name and address match the contractARES, Ministry of Finance
Not in liquidation or deletedliquidation guide
Who may sign for it (statutory body)Public register (Veřejný rejstřík), Ministry of Justice
VAT payer and not unreliableVAT register (Registr plátců DPH), Financial Administration
No insolvency proceedingsInsolvency register ISIR, Ministry of Justice
Website not on the risky e-shops listČOI list
VAT number valid for EU trade (foreign buyers)VIES

Before each payment: unreliable status and the bank account

  • Supplier still not unreliable (how to check).
  • The account on the invoice is published (how to check).
  • No unexplained change of bank details (fraud guide).

Ongoing: changes that should trigger a second look

  • New insolvency petitions, address, name or director changes.
  • Keep a record of each check with its date.
