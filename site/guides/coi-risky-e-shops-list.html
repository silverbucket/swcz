The ČOI risky e-shops list

Updated 22 October 2026 · about 3 minutes

The Czech Trade Inspection Authority (Česká obchodní inspekce, ČOI) publishes a list of websites it considers risky, mainly because they do not meet legal duties and consumer rights are hard to enforce against them.

Why ČOI puts a website on the risky e-shops list

According to ČOI, mainly for two reasons:

  • The website gives no contact details of the seller, so it is not clear who runs it or who you have a contract with.
  • The operator is known but based outside the EU, and the site lacks legally required information (such as terms), its terms grossly contradict the law, or ČOI has received many complaints about it.

ČOI says the list is a recommendation to think carefully before buying, that it is updated regularly, and that it is not a complete list of risky shops. A site that is not on it is not automatically safe.

How to check a website against the ČOI list

  1. Open the Risky e-shops list (Rizikové e-shopy), ČOI page.
  2. Search for the website's domain.
  3. Also check who runs the site: a Czech seller should give its IČO, which you can look up in ARES.
