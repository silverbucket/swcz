How to check a Czech supplier

Updated 21 October 2026 · about 5 minutes

Every Czech business has an 8-digit identification number, the IČO. With it you can check a supplier for free in four official sources. Here is what each one tells you and where to find it.

1. ARES: does the company exist, and is it still active?

ARES (Administrativní registr ekonomických subjektů) is the Ministry of Finance's search across the Czech public registers. Search by IČO or name and look at:

  • the exact legal name and registered address (sídlo), and whether they match the invoice;
  • the VAT ID (DIČ), usually "CZ" followed by the IČO for companies;
  • whether the company has a date of dissolution or "v likvidaci" (in liquidation) in its name;
  • links to the Commercial Register entry (or.justice.cz), where you find directors and filed documents such as annual accounts.

2. The VAT register: unreliable payer status and published bank accounts

The Financial Administration's register of VAT payers shows, for a DIČ:

  • whether the company is a VAT payer;
  • whether it is published as an unreliable VAT payer (nespolehlivý plátce) or an unreliable person (nespolehlivá osoba), and since when;
  • the list of bank accounts it has published.

These two items decide whether you can become liable for the supplier's VAT. Read our §109 guide for the rules. Check before each payment, because the status and the account list can change at any time.

3. ISIR: is the company in insolvency?

The insolvency register (ISIR) of the Ministry of Justice lists insolvency proceedings. Search by IČO. A filed insolvency petition does not always mean the company is bankrupt, but it is a strong reason to stop paying advances and to review open orders.

4. ČOI: is the supplier's e-shop on the risky websites list?

If you buy from a Czech online shop, check the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (ČOI) list of risky e-shops. ČOI lists websites it has received many complaints about or that it could not contact. It is aimed at consumers, but a listing is also a warning sign for a business buyer.
