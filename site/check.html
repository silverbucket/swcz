Supplier Watch CZ

Check one Czech supplier / Ověřit dodavatele

Free one-off check of a Czech company by IČO in the official registers: ARES (business register), the VAT register (unreliable payer status and published bank accounts), the insolvency register ISIR and, if you enter a website, the ČOI list of risky e-shops. No account needed.

Limited to a few checks per hour. Results come straight from the public registers at the moment you check; they are information, not legal or tax advice.
