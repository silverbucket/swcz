Data sources

Supplier Watch uses only official public sources. For each one: who publishes it, what we use it for, and the terms we use it under. We check every source each morning before the customer checks (see current status).

1. VAT register (Registr plátců DPH)

Published by: the Financial Administration of the Czech Republic (Finanční správa). We use: the public web service for third-party software (rozhraní CRPDPH): whether a supplier is published as an unreliable VAT payer (§106a VAT Act) and which bank accounts it has published (§98). Terms: public data published by law for exactly this purpose; free to use. Look up a supplier yourself.

2. ARES (business register data)

Published by: the Ministry of Finance. We use: the ARES REST API for the company name, registered address, legal form, dissolution and liquidation, and the public-register (veřejný rejstřík) record for the statutory body and owners. For directors and owners we store only a short fingerprint to detect changes, not the names. Terms: ARES data are public and published as open data; free to use. ares.gov.cz, details of companies at or.justice.cz.

3. Insolvency register (ISIR)

Published by: the Ministry of Justice. We use: the public ISIR web service to find insolvency proceedings by IČO, with the case number, state and a link to the case. We never store birth dates, personal ID numbers or home addresses. Terms: the register is public by law (§419 Insolvency Act); free to use within the service's limits (50 requests a minute). isir.justice.cz.

4. ČOI list of risky e-shops

Published by: the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (Česká obchodní inspekce, ČOI) as open data. We use: the daily CSV of risky websites, matched against the supplier websites you enter. If the list can't be downloaded, we use our saved copy if it is at most 7 days old, and the report shows its date. Terms: ČOI open-data licence, which allows commercial use with attribution and without implying endorsement: licence. Zdroj: Česká obchodní inspekce. Supplier Watch is not affiliated with or endorsed by ČOI. The list on coi.gov.cz.
