About Supplier Watch

Supplier Watch CZ checks Czech suppliers every morning in the official public registers and emails you, in English with the Czech wording alongside, only when something changes. Each month you also get a PDF report of every check, which you can keep as evidence that you checked your suppliers.

Why it exists

Under §109 of the Czech VAT Act a buyer can be made to pay a supplier's unpaid VAT if the supplier was published as an unreliable payer, or if the buyer paid to a bank account the supplier hadn't published. The registers are public and free, but checking them by hand for every supplier, every time, is easy to forget, and most tools are in Czech only. Supplier Watch does the checks automatically and keeps a record.

How we work

  • Official sources only. Every result comes from a public register; see data sources.
  • No false all-clears. A daily self-check tests every source. If a register is down or changes its format, the affected check is marked "not checked" in your alerts and report, never "OK".
  • Minimal data. We store your email, your supplier list and the check results. No tracking cookies, no ads. See the privacy policy.
  • Information, not advice. Results show what the registers said when we checked; they are not legal or tax advice.
