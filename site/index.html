What we check

Five checks, every morning, for every supplier on your list. All from official public sources.

Five checks for any supplier you look up with the free check. All from official public sources.

1. Unreliable VAT payer

Whether the tax office has published the supplier as an unreliable VAT payer. If you pay one, you can be made to pay its unpaid VAT (§109 VAT Act). Source: VAT register, Financial Administration

2. Published bank accounts

Which bank accounts the supplier has published to the tax office, and when they change. Paying to an account that isn't published can also make you liable for its VAT. Source: VAT register, Financial Administration

3. Insolvency (ISIR)

Whether the supplier is in insolvency proceedings. It's an early warning that deliveries, warranties or prepayments are at risk. Source: Insolvency Register, Ministry of Justice

4. Business register changes (ARES)

New registered address, a change of name, owners or statutory body as reflected in the register, liquidation or dissolution. Any of these can mean you're dealing with a different or ending company. Source: ARES, Ministry of Finance

5. Risky websites (ČOI)

Optional: add a supplier's website and we tell you if it appears on, or is removed from, the Czech Trade Inspection's list of risky e-shops. Source: Česká obchodní inspekce (open data, licence)

Monthly PDF report

On the 1st of each month you get a PDF listing every check we ran, per supplier, with the result and time. Keep it as evidence that you checked your suppliers. How each source is used

Supplier Watch is an independent service, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these authorities. If a register doesn't answer on a given day, we say so; we never report "all clear" for a check we couldn't run.

FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Only from official public registers: ARES (the Czech business register), the VAT register of the Financial Administration (mojedane.gov.cz) and ISIR (the insolvency register), plus, for suppliers with a website, the risky e-shop list published as open data by the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (Zdroj: Česká obchodní inspekce). We report what they say at the time we check; we are not a government service and this is not tax advice.

What is in the monthly report?

A PDF listing every check we ran during the month for each supplier: the time, and the result of each of the five checks (OK, alert, or not checked because a register was down), with details of any alert or change and the sources. It is emailed on the 1st and can be downloaded from your manage page. We keep the check history for 24 months.

I'm an accountant. Can I watch suppliers for several clients?

Yes. The Accountant plan (990 Kč / month, 2,000 suppliers in total) gives one login with a separate supplier list for each client. Each client list can have its own alert recipients and its own monthly report.

Is there an API?

Pro and Accountant plans include a read-only JSON API with your suppliers, their latest check results and alerts. See the API documentation.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime from your manage page (the link is in every email) using the Manage billing button. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid month, then moves to the free plan. To stop all emails, click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.

What do you do with my data?

We store your email address and your supplier list only to run the checks and send you alerts. No tracking cookies and no ads. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number. See the Privacy Policy.

Are the alerts only in English?

Alerts are written in English with the Czech wording alongside, so you can forward them to a Czech accountant.
