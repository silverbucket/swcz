Czech version below.

**What we process and why**
| Data | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your account: name, company, email, billing details | Providing the service, invoicing | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legal obligation for invoices (6(1)(c)) | Contract term; invoices 10 years (Czech accounting and tax law) |
| Your watchlist: company IDs, your labels, the account you pay | Running the checks | Contract (6(1)(b)) | Until you delete it or the contract ends + 30 days |
| Public register data about the subjects you monitor, including sole traders (name, registered address, VAT status, published accounts, insolvency case numbers and states) | Change detection and reports | Legitimate interest of you and us in checking business partners (6(1)(f)) | Latest snapshot; deleted with the watchlist |
| Check history: for each supplier and each daily check, the time and the result of each check (OK / alert / not checked, with the alert details) | Monthly PDF compliance report, API, evidence of your checks | Contract (6(1)(b)); legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) | 24 full months after the month of the check, then deleted automatically; stored monthly PDF reports likewise |
| Statutory body and owners of monitored companies | Detecting a change of directors or owners | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) | We store only a short one-way fingerprint, never the names |
| Accountant plan: client names, client supplier lists and the extra alert recipients' email addresses you enter | Sending that client's alerts and reports | Contract with you (6(1)(b)); our and your legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) | Until you remove them; each recipient can unsubscribe with their own link |
| API keys | Authenticating API requests | Contract (6(1)(b)) | Only a SHA-256 hash; revoked keys are kept as a record until the account is deleted |
| Technical logs (IP address, time, errors) | Security, debugging | Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) | 30 days |

**We don't** use tracking or advertising cookies, sell data, profile people, or store birth dates, personal ID numbers (rodné číslo) or home addresses from the insolvency register. We never send register data to anyone except you.

**Processors (recipients):** our server hosting provider (Linode), our email delivery provider Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), our mailbox provider mailbox.org (Heinlein Support GmbH, Germany) and our payment provider Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd, Ireland). Stripe may transfer payment data outside the EU/EEA under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

**Your rights:** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to contact@supplierwatch.cz. You may also complain to the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).

**Sole traders monitored by our customers:** your data come from public sources (ARES including the public register, the VAT payer register, ISIR and, for websites, the ČOI risky e-shop list). You can object at contact@supplierwatch.cz; we will assess the objection against our customer's legitimate interest.
